> - **Technology**: Keep Blue as primary; darken background to `#1A1A2E` for a dark-mode variant.

> - **Education**: Bright primaries, high contrast; ensure WCAG AA accessibility.
> - **Data-Driven**: Neutral background (`#FAFAFA`), use the full Google palette for chart differentiation.

## Layout Rules

- **Aspect Ratio**: 16:9 (standard widescreen)
- **Safe Margin**: 80 px on all sides — no content outside this zone
- **White Space**: Maintain at least 40% breathing room per slide; avoid clutter
- **Text Alignment**: Left-align body text; center only for hero numbers or title slides
- **Max Text per Slide**: 40 words for body copy; use visuals to replace paragraphs
- **Hierarchy**: One clear focal point per slide — title, stat, or image

---

## Visual Style

- **Icons**: Outlined, Google Material Icons style; 48×48 px minimum
- **Illustrations**: Flat design; use Google's illustration palette
- **Photography**: Bright, high-contrast; avoid dark or moody stock photos (unless Lifestyle/dark variant)
- **Charts & Graphs**: Clean axes, no gridline clutter; label data points directly
- **Dividers**: Use color blocks or thin `#E8EAED` lines — no drop shadows on content cards
